## 🎙️ Section 3: ElevenLabs / Voiceover Generation Settings

For the most impactful audio track, use these recommended parameters in your AI voice platform:

- **Voice Profile**: Professional, mid-pitch male or female tech presenter (e.g., *Adam*, *Rachel*, or *Charlie* in ElevenLabs).
- **Stability**: `0.72` (prevents emotional overshoots, keeps delivery steady and credible).
- **Clarity / Similarity Enhancement**: `0.85` (ensures technical terms like *'FAISS'*, *'SHAP'*, and *'SQL'* sound crisp).
- **Style Exaggeration**: `0.15` (authentic, confident keynote tone).
- **Speaking Rate**: `145 words per minute` (ideal pacing for a 5.5 minute video with visual breathers).
