AL-NOOR ACADEMY - EXAMINATION AND ASSESSMENT POLICY

1. Academic Year & Assessment Structure:
- The academic session runs from August to May, divided into two primary semesters:
  - First Term (Semester 1): Midterm Examinations in October, Term Finals in December.
  - Second Term (Semester 2): Midterm Assessments in March, Annual Final Board/School Examinations in May.
- Weightage breakdown: Continuous Assessment & Quizzes (20%), Midterm Exams (30%), Final Exams (50%).

2. Grading Scale and Criteria:
The school follows the standardized letter grading system:
- 90% and above : Grade A+ (Exceptional / High Distinction)
- 80% to 89.9%   : Grade A  (Excellent / Distinction)
- 70% to 79.9%   : Grade B  (Very Good / Above Average)
- 60% to 69.9%   : Grade C  (Satisfactory / Average)
- 50% to 59.9%   : Grade D  (Pass / Marginal)
- Below 50%      : Grade F  (Fail / Unsatisfactory)

3. Passing Standards:
- The minimum passing mark in each individual subject is 40% (40 out of 100).
- To qualify for promotion to the subsequent class, a student must pass in at least 5 out of 6 core subjects and achieve an aggregate percentage of not less than 50%.
- Core subjects include English, Urdu, Mathematics, Science, Islamiat, and Pakistan Studies.

4. Retake Examinations & Improvement Rules:
- A student failing in up to two subjects in the annual examination is eligible to sit for the Supplementary/Retake Examination scheduled in June.
- Retake examination fee is PKR 1,000 per subject.
- Retake examinations are evaluated on standard criteria; however, the maximum grade achievable on a retake exam is capped at Grade C (60%).
- Medical leave during examinations requires an authentic medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner within 48 hours. Eligible students may be granted an authorized makeup assessment.

5. Academic Integrity and Malpractice:
- Cheating, possession of unauthorized paper chits, digital smartwatches, or mobile phones during an exam results in immediate cancellation of that paper (Award of 0 marks).
- Severe offenses will lead to a 1-year suspension and a recorded disciplinary notice.
