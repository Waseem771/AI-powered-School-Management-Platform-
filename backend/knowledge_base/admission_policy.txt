AL-NOOR ACADEMY - ADMISSION & ENROLLMENT POLICY

1. Eligibility & Age Requirements (as of 1st August of the academic year):
- Grade 6: Minimum 10 years, maximum 12 years of age.
- Grade 7: Minimum 11 years, maximum 13 years of age.
- Grade 8: Minimum 12 years, maximum 14 years of age.
- Grade 9: Minimum 13 years, maximum 15 years of age.
- Grade 10: Minimum 14 years, maximum 16 years of age (Transfers only with valid Board registration).

2. Required Admission Documents:
All prospective student applications must be submitted with:
- Original NADRA B-Form (Child Registration Certificate) and 2 photocopies.
- Attested copy of Father / Guardian's Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC).
- Original School Leaving Certificate (SLC) / Transfer Certificate from previous registered school.
- Certified transcript / report card of the previous grade with passing marks.
- 4 passport-size photographs with light blue background.
- Medical fitness and vaccination history record.

3. Admission Assessment & Entrance Test:
- Candidates seeking admission into Grades 6 to 9 must pass an entrance test covering English, Mathematics, and Science/Urdu.
- Qualifying benchmark is 50% aggregate in the written entrance test.
- A brief formal interview with the candidate and parents is conducted prior to final seat confirmation.

4. Transfer and Migration Rules:
- Inter-board and inter-city migrations require a No Objection Certificate (NOC) and countersigned School Leaving Certificate by the relevant District Education Authority.
- Admissions into Grade 10 are strictly subject to seat vacancy and prior Federal/Provincial Board registration compatibility.
- Admission registration fee is PKR 5,000 (one-time, non-refundable).
