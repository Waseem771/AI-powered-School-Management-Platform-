AL-NOOR ACADEMY - FEE POLICY AND REGULATIONS

1. Fee Schedule and Due Dates:
- Tuition fees are billed on a monthly basis across 12 calendar months.
- The standard monthly tuition fee is PKR 3,500 for all secondary grades (Grades 6 through 10).
- Examination fee is PKR 500 per semester, billed semi-annually (in October and March).
- All fee challans are issued on the 1st of each calendar month.
- The standard due date for payment without penalty is the 10th of each month.

2. Late Fee Surcharges:
- Payments made between the 11th and 15th of the month incur a flat late fee surcharge of PKR 200.
- For payments delayed past the 15th of the month, a fine of PKR 50 per day is applied.
- If fees remain unpaid for more than 45 days after the due date, the student's name may be struck off the active school roll, and a re-admission fee of PKR 2,000 will be required to reactivate enrollment.

3. Fee Refund Rules:
- Admission fees, registration charges, and laboratory fees are strictly non-refundable under any circumstances.
- If a student withdraws prior to the official commencement of the academic term, 80% of the tuition fee paid in advance will be refunded upon written application.
- If withdrawal takes place within the first 14 calendar days after classes begin, 50% of tuition fee is refundable.
- No refund of any fee or charges will be issued if a student leaves or withdraws after 14 calendar days of the term.
- Security deposits (if applicable) are 100% refundable upon presentation of clearance form signed by all department heads.

4. Fee Concessions & Scholarships:
- Sibling Concession: When two or more siblings are enrolled concurrently, the second child receives a 25% waiver on monthly tuition fees, and the third child receives a 50% waiver.
- Merit Scholarship: Top academic performer in each grade section (securing 90%+ in the annual examination) receives a 100% tuition fee waiver for the subsequent academic term.
- Need-Based Assistance: Needy families may apply for Zakat/Bait-ul-Maal financial assistance committee by submitting income documentation before September 15 each year.

5. Payment Methods:
- Payments can be made via authorized bank branches (Allied Bank Limited, Meezan Bank) using official 3-copy printed challan vouchers.
- Online banking, 1Link, and mobile wallet transfers (Easypaisa / JazzCash) are accepted using the designated 8-digit Challan Number.
- Cash payments are accepted at the school accounts desk between 8:30 AM and 1:30 PM on official working days.
