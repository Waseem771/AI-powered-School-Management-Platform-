AL-NOOR ACADEMY - CODE OF CONDUCT, UNIFORM & DISCIPLINE

1. School Timings & Punctuality:
- Regular school timings: Monday to Thursday: 7:50 AM to 1:45 PM. Friday: 7:50 AM to 12:30 PM.
- Gates close promptly at 8:00 AM. Students arriving after 8:00 AM are marked late.
- Three consecutive late arrivals lead to a formal warning and parent notification.

2. School Uniform Regulations:
- Summer Uniform (Boys): Sky-blue short/long-sleeve shirt with embroidered school emblem, navy blue trousers, black socks, and polished black Oxford shoes.
- Summer Uniform (Girls): Sky-blue kameez with school monogram, white shalwar, white dupatta/scarf, white socks, and flat black school shoes.
- Winter Uniform: Navy blue blazer or official school V-neck sweater with maroon school tie.
- Friday Attire: White Shalwar Kameez with green house waistcoat / white sash.
- Strict prohibition on improper haircuts, fancy wristbands, and jewelry.

3. Attendance & Leave Rules:
- A minimum of 75% overall attendance is legally mandatory to sit for terminal and annual board examinations.
- Sick leave exceeding 2 days must be accompanied by a formal medical certificate.
- Unexcused absence of more than 6 consecutive days results in cancellation of enrollment.
- Leave requests must be submitted in writing signed by parents via the school communication diary or email.

4. Prohibited Items and Disciplinary Codes:
- Mobile phones, smartwatches, recording devices, and gaming gadgets are strictly banned inside campus premises. Confiscated phones are kept for 30 days and incur a PKR 1,000 release fine.
- Bullying, physical aggression, disrespect toward teachers or staff, vandalism, and foul language carry zero-tolerance disciplinary action including immediate expulsion.
