The Critical Administrative Pain Points in K-12 & Higher Ed

Schools generate thousands of records, yet staff spend hours digging through fragmented systems while struggling students slip through unnoticed.

1

Administrative Data Silos

Staff waste 3–5 hours daily running custom SQL queries or exporting CSVs just to answer simple operational questions like "Which students in Class 10 have overdue fees?"

Result: Delayed fee collections & operational paralysis
2

Unanswered Policy Queries

Parents, teachers, and admissions officers drown in 100+ page handbooks. They struggle to find exact refund rules, attendance thresholds, and exam deferral criteria.

Lagging Dropout Detection

Result: Avoidable academic failure & high attrition

Slide 03 / 06 • Architecture Pillars

Four Core AI Innovations in EduCore

Built with enterprise security, zero hallucination RAG, and safe SQL tool-calling.

1

Grounded Policy RAG

FAISS Vector Store + Strict Confidence Filtering

Ingests the full school code of conduct and policy handbook. Embeds sections into FAISS. Answers policy queries with exact handbook citations and zero hallucination fallback.

Query: "What is the fee refund policy?" → "Section 4.2: 100% refund within 14 days..."
2

Safe SQL Tool Calling

Groq LLaMA • Zero Direct SQL Injection

LLM never writes arbitrary raw SQL. Instead, it dispatches verified programmatic tools: get_student_profile, get_fee_summary, and get_students_with_pending_fees.

Safe execution layer: Parameterized ORM queries with read-only validation.
3

Multi-Turn Context Memory

Automatic Entity Tracking & Pronoun Resolution

Staff can inspect a student by name, and subsequently ask "What are his pending fees?" or "What is her attendance?" The system preserves conversational context across turns.

Stateful memory toggle: 🧠 Memory: ON retains previous student entity.
4

Explainable AI Risk Engine

RandomForest Classifier + SHAP Contribution

Evaluates grades, attendance rate, assignment completion, and disciplinary logs. Flags at-risk students with explainable factors (e.g. "-42% risk due to low attendance").

Actionable interventions: Counselors receive proactive trigger alerts.

Judge Question 1

“How do you prevent the LLM from hallucinating policy rules?”

Winning Answer: We don't rely on the model's parametric memory. We embed the official school handbook in FAISS. The assistant is strictly system-prompted to cite source policy sections, and if confidence is low, it explicitly refuses to invent rules.

Judge Question 2

“How do you protect the database from prompt injection?”

Winning Answer: The model never generates raw SQL. It dispatches safe Python tool calls with structured parameters. The backend executes parameterized ORM queries through an allowlisted read-only interface.

Judge Question 3

“What is your business model and market viability?”

Winning Answer: B2B SaaS subscription per school (e.g. $1.50/student/month). Schools recoup the cost within 30 days simply through accelerated fee recovery and automated parent support ticket reduction.
